# DEAD AIR — Ending & Disclosure Structure

**Status:** Working narrative contract v0.1

## 1. Governing rule
The ending may close the player's work order; it must not close the mystery. Never confirm what lies beyond the connection, what it wants, or whether experimental AI caused the incident.

## 2. First-release ending: No Verified Link
### Trigger
The player submits A-006 after the essential evidence set is available: NR-041 registry response, first and recurring retained events, M-07, M-08, and M-09. If the player skipped an item, the archive recovery route surfaces it before submission. No tuning, recording duration, optional branch, or theory selection is required.

### Sequence
1. Player reviews established observations, source records, unresolved questions, and recommended action.
2. Player submits a report stating the records conflict without selecting which record is wrong.
3. Mara Venn responds: “Received. No verified registered-station link is established by the material available to this office. The chronology question remains open pending source-clock documentation. The report is accepted for filing; this acceptance does not confirm the underlying event.”
4. The report is permanently stored as a new record with its own ID and source links.
5. Assignment status becomes CLOSED. The case thread remains OPEN / UNRESOLVED.
6. A final handover note indicates that the retained event index has been refreshed. It contains the same NR-041 identifier and sequence 731904 with no new explanatory metadata. This is a recurrence of evidence, not an apparition or UI malfunction.
7. End card: “SHIFT CLOSED — RECORDS RETAINED — SOURCE UNRESOLVED.”

### What changes
The current assignment closes; report and review are saved; notebook can continue; a follow-up may be offered.

### What does not change
Original M-07/M-08/M-09 wording, timestamp fields, missing registry entry, ambiguity around sequence/source, and unknown relationship to the research-facility incident remain unchanged.

## 3. Player expression
Allow a recommended administrative action:
- Retain records and request clock/source documentation.
- Request independent technical review.
- Close this report with source attribution unresolved.

Choices affect report wording and later message tone only. They cannot erase evidence or establish unsupported truth. No morality score.

## 4. Future variation (deferred)
Later variations may reflect whether the player preserved recordings, linked document copies, or documented uncertainty. They may affect follow-up work, never essential evidence availability.

Do not add an ending that proves AI caused the incident, identifies an entity, explains what lies beyond the connection, or proves deliberate falsification.

## 5. Acceptance tests
- Reachable without Silent Site content.
- Essential evidence recoverable before submission.
- Report accepted while source and chronology remain unresolved.
- Primary incident documents remain unchanged after closure.
- Final recurrence is a normal retained event with provenance and transcript.
- Player can continue or close the app without penalty.
