# DEAD AIR — Master Chronology & Evidence Ledger

*Working story-bible supplement, version 0.1, 27 October 2026.*

## 1. Canon and Uncertainty Rules

- Separate claimed event time, document creation time, transmission/receipt time, and archive-ingestion or correction time. These fields may disagree.
- A document proves that a record was made or a claim entered; it does not automatically prove that the claim is true.
- No source currently establishes the true order of the original incident's shutdown, evacuation, communications, or reporting.
- Do not reveal what lies beyond the connection, whether it has intentions, or whether the experimental AI caused the incident.
- Administrative closure is a recurring institutional pattern, not proof of a supernatural force.
- Player-facing documents must each remain individually plausible; contradictions should emerge through comparison.
- Essential evidence must not be missable because of a short recording, receiver settings, a missed transmission, or one early choice.
- Unfixed dates, times, frequencies, call signs, and numeric sequences remain explicit placeholders.

## 5. Silent Site — Optional Evidence Ledger

### Branch outcome

**Resolved:** an administrative closure package was dispatched and later indexed as received; the surviving inventory does not list the missing operational logs.

**Unresolved:** whether the logs were separately transferred, never inventoried, retained elsewhere, or never included. No evidence establishes deliberate destruction or a connection to the main incident. Mara accepts the report as a records discrepancy and recommends retention under standard procedure.

### Silent Site guardrails

- Do not imply deliberate destruction, a cover-up, or a main-story connection.
- The obsolete stamp and illegible initials are administrative uncertainties, not proof of wrongdoing.
- The player's defensible conclusion is limited: the package trail exists, but the surviving inventory does not account for the operational logs.
- The branch closes administratively while the media disposition remains unresolved.

## 7. Player Knowledge by Depth

- **Surface:** NR-041 is unregistered and recurs; a closed incident file references its sequence; shutdown chronology conflicts.
- **Moderate:** an independent communications record repeats the sequence after shutdown; administrative closure does not settle the evidence.
- **Deep:** personnel records, missing pages, calibration similarities, and independent records broaden the pattern, but do not establish which documents are wrong or who altered/misfiled them.
- **Optional Silent Site:** transfer package was received, but inventory does not account for missing logs; their fate remains unknown.

## 8. Data Integrity Rules

Every artifact needs a unique stable ID, source, author/responsible office if known, claimed event timestamp, creation timestamp, archive timestamp, status, and version history. Original document text is immutable. Corrections are new linked records, never silent edits.

Every clue needs a discovery route and a recovery route. If a live event is missed, archive or cross-station evidence must preserve critical progression. Preserve conflicting records rather than selecting a winner. Better tuning may recover missing words, but cannot resolve contradictory documents. High decoding confidence means an identifier was read reliably—not that the registry or identity is correct.

Mara's statements must reflect administrative criteria and available records; she is neither a supernatural oracle nor automatically a villain. Side stories may echo themes but should have independent evidence and plausible ordinary explanations unless deliberately left open.

## 10. Chronology Audit Checklist

- Can every dated document fit without accidentally resolving the intended contradiction?
- Is each timestamp clearly event, creation, receipt, archive, or correction time?
- Does each report state only what the player could know at that progression point?
- Are repeated codes, station IDs, and frequencies consistent?
- Can each essential clue be recovered after its original transmission is missed?
- Does every official closure end an administrative task without falsely resolving the mystery?
- Do optional branches have their own satisfying evidence chain and outcome?
- Does the final story refuse to identify what lies beyond the connection or confirm AI responsibility?
